Bre: Designer & Creator

Project Description

This section provides a detailed description of the project, highlighting the key features and artistic vision behind the pictures.

Image 1: The Flower Pot

This artwork showcases a vibrant flower pot rendered in a rich palette derived from a mixture of yellow, blue, red, and white pigments. A key focus of this piece was the exploration of shadow, evident in the deliberate application of black along the side of the second pot, adding a sense of depth and dimension to the composition.

Image 2: The Pineapple

This sketch of a pineapple possesses a unique quality, born from the sensory experience of enjoying the fruit while simultaneously capturing its form on paper. It's a drawing directly influenced by the act of observation and taste.

Image 3: The Eye Spy Art

Created from the very same colorful droplets of paint that formed "The Flower Pot," this intriguing piece was brought into existence through a process of folding. The resulting abstract patterns hold hidden figures, inviting the viewer to discover the many animals concealed within. The artist has identified a goat and a bird amongst the forms.

Image 4: The Rose and Butterfly

This "pop out" sketch of a rose accompanied by a butterfly was brought to life using thick paper and markers. The technique suggests a dimensional quality, where elements of the drawing might lift or stand out from the surface, creating a tactile and visually engaging effect.

Image 5: WHAT IF HOME IS A SHOE

This painting is described as an "inner child painting," evoking a sense of nostalgia and the boundless curiosity of youth. It specifically recalls a time when the artist frequently posed "What If" questions to their parents, suggesting a whimsical and imaginative concept exploring unconventional ideas of home.

Image 6: The Shadowy Landscape

This drawing, executed purely with pencil, emphasizes the level of detail achievable through this medium. The scene depicts a tranquil riverside setting, where a waterfall dramatically cascades between two imposing cliffs, highlighting the interplay of light and shadow within the natural landscape.

Image 7: Koi Fish Sketch

The image is described as a detailed sketch featuring one or more koi fish. The text suggests that the drawing's style indicates it could serve as a preliminary study for a larger work, such as a painting or a tattoo design, or simply be an artistic exploration of the subject.

Image 8: Humming Bird

This drawing features the Hummingbird, the National Bird of Jamaica. The artist created this piece as a heartfelt expression of longing for home.
